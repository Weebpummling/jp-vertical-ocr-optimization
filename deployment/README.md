# Layer 7 — Unit deployment & location

There is no single feed that says "Lt. A took his company to Manchuria for a year." The
question decomposes into two links, one easy and one bounded:

1. **Individual → unit** *(well recorded, automatable)* — roster snapshots record 職名/部隊,
   and Kanpō 補職/転任 orders give dated assignment changes. This falls out of data already
   being captured.
2. **Unit → location** *(curated reference table)* — heterogeneous sources, but **finite**:
   ~21 standing divisions and their regiments. Sources: 陣中日誌 survivors at JACAR, 聯隊史,
   衛戍地 references, 戦時編制 compilations, 戦史叢書, and order-of-battle references as an index.

**Build at division granularity first** — Manchuria deployment in this era largely occurred at
division level via a documented rotation system, which makes division-year location tractable.
Refine to regiment where sources allow. Grade confidence; cite sources. Cross-check against
Kanpō 補職 orders, which partially reconstruct unit movement from the personnel side.

This is substantively central, not a side quest: where a unit was stationed is part of every
member officer's career record, and theater postings are among the events the panel must date.

**Individual-level cross-check (researched July 2026):** see `crosscheck-sources.md` —
the 満洲事変論功行賞 roll (serialized in Kanpō 附録, April–May 1935, pids ≈
2958952–2959005) names individual officers decorated for Incident service, upgrading
"his unit went" to "he demonstrably served" for the decorated subset; regimental
histories' 将校職員表 add a second per-officer confirmation; restricted service records
(兵籍簿) serve as spot-validation only. 従軍記章 has no recipient roll — not minable.
